## Global constraints

Preserve historical amounts, passwords and user .workbuddy/. All writes are scoped POST/CSRF and atomic. New cross-org permissions use explicit routes. No physical deletion of historical business rows. Uploads remain private. GitHub existing branch/PR stays the delivery destination.

## Review focus

- Removed/invalid/oversize attachments and duplicate imports never bypass authorization or leave partial business writes.
