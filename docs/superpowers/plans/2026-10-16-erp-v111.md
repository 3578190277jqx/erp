## Ledger

Ruling: Use explicit source→factory collaboration routes rather than user memberships for N1. Membership would grant excessive target-side visibility and prevent normal source-only sales workflows.
Ruling: Keep original contract amounts and historical line totals; backfill discount from immutable source quotes, and do not recompute approved changes.

Ruling: Do not reprice historical approved change lines using the new contract discount. New lines store a net_amount snapshot; legacy approved/rejected rows with no snapshot display a historical-basis note and refer to approval audit.
