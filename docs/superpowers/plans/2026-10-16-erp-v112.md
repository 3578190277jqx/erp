# ERP v1.1.2 Implementation Plan

> Execute inline with superpowers:executing-plans and test-driven-development. One independent whole-change reviewer at completion.

**Goal:** Remove one-way workflow locks while preserving authorization, pricing history and posted receipts.
**Architecture:** Extend existing FSM and focused services; no new runtime dependency. Persist explicit change intent and discount provenance.
**Tech Stack:** Django5.2, Decimal/integer cents, SQLite, PostgreSQL16, Chrome/Playwright.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-erp-v112-design.md

## Global constraints

Preserve historical totals, posted Payment records, source quotes, audit and user .workbuddy/. Source-order-first lock order; scoped POST/CSRF writes, required reason/version for withdrawals. Do not grant target data access with collaboration permission. Keep 404 and existing receiver role separation.

## Review focus

- An unresponsive/disabled factory can never permanently lock an unaccepted source order.
- Concurrent accept/withdraw commits only one outcome, never a withdrawn handoff with a factory job.
- Repricing with receipts never reduces a period below its allocation or moves explicit/FIFO receipts.
- Unknown historical discount and missing product snapshots cannot silently invent new prices.
- Handoff evidence becomes inaccessible to the factory after withdrawal; unrelated users never see it.

## Ledger

Ruling: Resize outstanding balances rather than reapply original gross percentages, so already allocated deposits/tail receipts are preserved and settled periods stay settled. If the customer later wants a different policy, it is a new explicit business rule.
Ruling: Keep Handoff effects in collaboration services but make their legal transitions and displayed actions part of the shared FSM; do not weaken global cross-org scope.
