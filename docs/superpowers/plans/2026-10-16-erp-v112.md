## Global constraints

Preserve historical totals, posted Payment records, source quotes, audit and user .workbuddy/. Source-order-first lock order; scoped POST/CSRF writes, required reason/version for withdrawals. Do not grant target data access with collaboration permission. Keep 404 and existing receiver role separation.

## Review focus

- An unresponsive/disabled factory can never permanently lock an unaccepted source order.
- Concurrent accept/withdraw commits only one outcome, never a withdrawn handoff with a factory job.
- Repricing with receipts never reduces a period below its allocation or moves explicit/FIFO receipts.
- Unknown historical discount and missing product snapshots cannot silently invent new prices.
- Handoff evidence becomes inaccessible to the factory after withdrawal; unrelated users never see it.
